## 3. Model weights

`backend/ml/python_service/department_model/` and `multi_intent_model/` (stored with Git LFS) are the team's own fine-tunes of **DistilBERT** (`distilbert-base-uncased`, Hugging Face), which is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**. Base model © the Hugging Face team and contributors; see https://huggingface.co/distilbert-base-uncased and https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0. The fine-tuned weights are redistributed under Apache-2.0 with this notice; the modifications are fine-tuning on the team's own synthetic helpdesk training samples. These weights belong to the underlying OneGate helpdesk; the SME copilot does not use them (its guard is rule-based).

## 5. Demo video narration

The voiceover of the demo video (not part of this repository) was synthesised offline with **Piper** (open-source TTS by the Rhasspy project) using the voice `en_US-lessac-medium` from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices. That voice's model card names its training data as the Lessac / Blizzard Challenge 2013 corpus, whose licence (https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html) permits research use and excludes commercial use. The video is a non-commercial competition entry; the voice model is not redistributed here. A commercial version would use a voice with a commercial licence or a human narrator.
